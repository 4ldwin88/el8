What is EL8?

A wellness operating system for your whole life.

EL8 is being built to help people improve their lives across eight connected dimensions of wellness: physical, emotional, intellectual, social, spiritual, occupational, financial, and environmental. It learns what is happening, identifies what may deserve attention, confirms what you want to focus on, and builds a realistic Plan around your evidence, capacity, preferences, and circumstances.

Discover → Prioritize → Confirm Focus → Plan → Act → Review → Adjust

You are testing EL8 — not yourself.

  • There are no right or wrong answers.
  • You can answer as yourself or roleplay someone different.
  • Watch for irrelevant, repeated, confusing, or missing questions and recommendations.
  • Use Tester Notes whenever something seems wrong.
