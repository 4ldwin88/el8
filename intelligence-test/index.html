What is EL8?

A wellness operating system for your whole life.

EL8 is being built to help people improve their lives across eight connected dimensions of wellness: physical, emotional, intellectual, social, spiritual, occupational, financial, and environmental. Instead of giving everyone the same checklist, EL8 learns what is happening in your life, works out what deserves attention first, and builds a realistic plan around your priorities, capacity, and circumstances.

Over time, the goal is for EL8 to keep learning from check-ins and results so the plan can adapt as your life changes.

Understand → Prioritize → Plan → Learn → Adapt

You are testing EL8 — not yourself.

  • There are no right or wrong answers. Don't try to guess what EL8 wants you to say.
  • You can answer as yourself or roleplay someone different if you would rather not share truthful personal information. If you roleplay, stay reasonably consistent.
  • Watch for questions that don't make sense based on earlier answers, irrelevant or repeated questions, important things EL8 misses, or recommendations that seem wrong.
  • Every meaningful step has a Tester Notes box. Use it whenever you find a bug, confusing wording, repetition, or anything else worth telling us.

About this research test

Your responses and interactions are recorded anonymously to help improve EL8. Please don't enter your name, email, or other identifying information in free-text notes. Wellness answers can still be sensitive, so only share what you're comfortable testing.
