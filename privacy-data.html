Record integrity

Submitted assessments, Daily Check-ins and Weekly Check-ins are historical records and cannot be edited after submission. Tracking records use a correction/revision pathway when information needs to be corrected.

Capture media

Photos and voice captures are temporary unless you explicitly choose to keep the original media during tracking. Confirmed structured records and interpretations may remain in your EL8 history.

Data controls

Download/export and production account-deletion workflows are not yet exposed in this prototype. They must be implemented before EL8 is treated as a production member service.
