# Discovery Multi-Signal Resolution

This branch is rebuilt from current `main` to replace the conflicted experimental branch while preserving the accepted Multi-Signal Resolution architecture.

Core rule: every material member-raised signal must be accounted for before Discovery closes. Signals may resolve, link to an upstream explanation, clear through contradictory/corrective evidence, or explicitly defer at the fatigue ceiling; they may not disappear silently.

The question selector ranks questions across unresolved signals. Signal state and stopping logic remain separate from question utility. The original finance + health/energy human-test failure is retained as a permanent regression scenario.
